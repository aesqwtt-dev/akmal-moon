05 — The Making · Darkness

Every piece begins in the dark.

Inspired by the elegance of the moon — calm, timeless, and unforgettable — our shop was created to bring together beautiful design, quality craftsmanship, and a personal touch.

Crescent

The first light is the cut.

Patterns are drafted by hand and cut one at a time. A single tailor follows each garment from the first chalk line to the final press — never an assembly line.

Half moon

Hope is construction.

When I was creating this website, I hoped it would become an elegant and beautiful reflection of my dad’s clothing shop. I wanted every detail to feel stylish, modern, and meaningful. My goal was not just to create a website, but to create something that would represent the beauty, quality, and passion behind site

Full moon

Revealed only when complete.

When I finally completed the website, I decided not to reveal it to my dad right away. I wanted to keep it a surprise and see his genuine reaction when he saw it for the first time. I spent a lot of time working on every detail, from the design and colors to the smallest elements, because I wanted the website to feel special and truly represent site
